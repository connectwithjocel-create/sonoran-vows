Planning considerations

Wine country, well planned.

What to think about when celebrating in Sonoita and Elgin.

Arizona's first wine region

The Sonoita AVA was established in 1984 as Arizona's first American Viticultural Area, covering grasslands between the Santa Rita, Huachuca and Whetstone mountains.

Elevation & weather

The area sits well above Tucson, which often means milder summer temperatures, and cooler evenings and earlier frosts in the fall. Layers and lighting matter after sunset.

Travel & lodging

Sonoita and Elgin are rural communities about an hour's drive from Tucson. Guest transportation and lodging plans deserve early attention.

Vineyard settings

Tasting rooms and vineyard properties each have their own rules for events, catering and timing. Venue relationships are confirmed individually.
